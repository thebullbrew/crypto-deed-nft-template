**ASSIGNMENT OF MEMBERSHIP INTEREST**

This Assignment ("Assignment") is entered into as of ____________, 20___,
by and between:

- **Assignor:** ______________________________ ("Assignor")
- **Assignee:** ______________________________ ("Assignee")

**Recitals**

A. Assignor is the sole member of ______________________________ LLC,
   a __________________ limited liability company (the "Company"), which holds
   legal title to the real property described in Exhibit A (the "Property").
B. Assignor is the holder of DeedNFT token ID ______ (contract
   ______________________________, Ethereum mainnet), which represents 100%
   of the membership interest in the Company.
C. Assignor wishes to assign, and Assignee wishes to acquire, 100% of the
   membership interest in the Company, with the on-chain transfer of the token
   serving as the digital counterpart of this Assignment.

**Agreement**

1. **Assignment.** Assignor hereby assigns to Assignee 100% of Assignor's
   membership interest in the Company, including all rights to distributions,
   allocations, and management of the Company.
2. **Token transfer.** Contemporaneous with execution, Assignor shall transfer
   DeedNFT token ID ______ to Assignee's address ______________________.
   The parties agree the token transfer and this Assignment are counterparts
   of a single transaction; neither is effective without the other.
3. **Deed anchor.** The token is anchored to the recorded deed identified by
   SHA-256 `______________________________`, recorded as
   ______________________________ with __________________ County.
4. **Representations.** Assignor represents it (a) is the sole member of the
   Company, (b) holds the token free of liens, (c) has authority to assign,
   and (d) the Company holds marketable title to the Property subject only
   to the encumbrances listed in Exhibit B (title commitment attached).
5. **Operating agreement.** Assignee agrees to be bound by the Company's
   operating agreement as the substituted sole member.
6. **Taxes and filings.** The parties shall cooperate on transfer-tax filings,
   LLC records updates, and any required notices. [Counsel: address
   jurisdiction-specific transfer tax on membership-interest sales.]
7. **Governing law.** This Assignment is governed by the laws of
   __________________.

**Exhibit A** — Legal description of the Property (exactly as on the recorded deed)
**Exhibit B** — Title commitment / encumbrances
